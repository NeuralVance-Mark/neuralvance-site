AI Governance Methodology

The Agentic Trust
Threshold Framework

Every AI action exists on two axes: reversibility and stakes. This determines where human judgment is required.

Low Stakes · Reversible

Full Autonomy

AI operates independently without human review. Actions can be undone if incorrect and consequences are limited.

E.g. Content scheduling, meeting reminders, research summaries

High Stakes · Reversible

Review Recommended

AI drafts and recommends. Human reviews before action is taken. 30-second oversight prevents meaningful errors.

E.g. Client communications, proposal drafts, vendor evaluations

Low Stakes · Irreversible

Audit Trail Required

AI may act but all decisions are logged with full traceability. Regular review cadence ensures alignment.

E.g. Data classification, automated reporting, system configurations

High Stakes · Irreversible

Human Approval Required

No AI action proceeds without explicit human authorization. This threshold is non-negotiable regardless of confidence level.

E.g. Board communications, contract execution, security decisions
